# Create and share a post

Open **Create**, choose photos or video clips, then tap **Next**. Select each
photo to crop, adjust its appearance, add text or draw. Tap **Next** to write a
caption and choose who can see your post. Photos prepare and upload while you
write. Tap **Share** when ready.

Upload progress appears above Share. If the connection fails, **Retry upload**
keeps photos that already finished. Editing a prepared photo uploads its new
version. Saving a draft keeps a private server copy; device drafts recover after
a reload on the same browser/account. A browser must remain open during a normal
post's upload. The separate Instagram ZIP background uploader is unchanged.

Advanced settings contain alt text and scheduling. Story creation includes
existing polls and HTTPS links. Reels retain their existing rendering controls
and require the server's configured FFmpeg/FFprobe helpers. There is no licensed
music catalog, collaborator tagging or Live streaming in this preview.

The home header has Apps and Notifications. Settings and theme are available
from your profile. A post's **…** menu contains its audience and the actions you
can take. Private originals remain in the protected My Photos/My Social store.
